## Contemporaneous observations and evidence gaps

An eligible explicit consultation captures a separate current TASK episode cut
before optional advice and checks it again afterward. A `PilotAdviceObservation`

quality. Receipts store no advice prose, later outcomes, labels or reference answers.

accepts at most 40 contemporaneous sealed receipt objects. It reauthenticates their

TASK revision/RUN/terminal/review/publication and safe failure/ref source facts

The report is strict bounded JSON, at most 128 KiB. Source failure, ref/budget

the existing caller may display the safe report through its normal observation
means. No persistence is added. If original authenticated receipt objects are no
longer available, pre-outcome advice remains UNKNOWN. Later episode cuts cannot
enrich earlier receipts or reconstruct advice retrospectively.
