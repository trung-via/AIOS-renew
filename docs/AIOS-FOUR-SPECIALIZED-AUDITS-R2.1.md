Normative substitution would require separate Human policy and conformance approval.

No Stage-1-specialized + Stage-2-V1 mixing, no mid-attempt profile switching, no silent V1/V3 fallback. A fresh Human decision can authorize a newly bound audit, without rewriting previous work.
